    Loyalty strategy: the value proposition and the analytics roadmap behind it

    Majid Al Futtaim (Carrefour)

    A stronger member offer and one analytics vision across 15 markets, lifting the share of sales made by scanned loyalty members.

    LoyaltyValue PropositionSales PenetrationAnalytics StrategyExecutive Communication

    Customer segmentation: where every Carrefour shopper sits

    Majid Al Futtaim (Carrefour)

    26 machine-learning segments show who each shopper is, what they value, how and when they shop, and what they need.

    Machine LearningTransactional DataRFMPower BISegmentation
    Problem & approach

    • Carrefour served one customer base made up of very different shoppers

  6. Collections Strategy2016–2018
    75% → 80%Delinquent-card resolution rate

    Cyclical allocation for credit-card collections

    Emirates NBD

    Moved delinquent-card allocation from once a month to ten cycles, with the best-matched agent for each customer.

    CollectionsMachine LearningAllocation StrategyAutomationSAS
    Problem & approach
    Problem
    • Delinquent cards were allocated to agents once a month, on the 1st
    • A customer who missed a payment on the 2nd waited up to 29 days for follow-up
    • Resolution stalled at 75%, and provisions were higher than needed
    Approach
    • Redesigned allocation into ten cycles a month, grouping statement dates so each cycle carried equal customers
    • Matched each customer with the best available agent
    • Contacted likely defaulters early with a machine-learning pre-delinquency model
    • Auto-called customers during the first five days of delinquency, avoiding agent allocation and incentives
    • Issued a daily automated paid/unpaid report, giving agents a fresh list every morning
    Outcome
    Resolution rate rose from 75% to 80%.
  7. Predictive AI2016–2018
    Random ForestScores every card account on its chance of missing the next due date

    Pre-delinquency model: predicting who will miss the next due date

    Emirates NBD

    A Random Forest classifier that scores every card account on its probability of missing the next due date, so collectors can contact customers before they become delinquent.

    Random ForestMachine LearningPre-delinquencyPythonCollections
    Problem & approach
    Problem
    • Collections started only after an account was already delinquent
    • Customers likely to miss their next due date looked the same as those who would pay
    • Contacting every customer was too costly
    Approach
    • Built a Random Forest classifier, an ensemble of decision trees trained on bootstrap samples
    • Predicted the probability that each card account would miss its next due date
    • Randomised the features at each split to reduce overfitting
    • Tuned hyperparameters on a hold-out set and checked out-of-bag error
    • Explained the drivers with feature importance
    • Banded scores into risk deciles and fed them into the monthly allocation cycles and auto-calling
    Outcome
    Collectors contacted the highest-risk customers before the due date, instead of after the miss.
  8. Optimisation2018–2021
    AED 1MSaved per year on SMS communicationTamayuz Award · 2020

    SMS communication cost optimisation

    Abu Dhabi Commercial Bank (ADCB)

    Machine-learning targeting that sends an SMS only where it changes the outcome, cutting spend without hurting results.

    Machine LearningUplift ModellingOptimisationCustomer Targeting
    Problem & approach
    Problem
    • Untargeted SMS drove high spend
    • English and Arabic messages exceeded one SMS segment (160 and 70 characters), so each was billed twice
    • Unnecessary reminders went out as soon as a statement was generated
    • Many messages reached customers who would have acted anyway
    Approach
    • Separated mandatory messages from discretionary ones
    • Measured each SMS's incremental effect against a no-message control group
    • Modelled each customer's likely response using machine learning
    • Sent only where the message changed the outcome
    • Ranked customers by value per SMS cost
    Outcome
    AED 1M saved per year, with outcomes held against the control group.
