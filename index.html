About

I turn customer data into commercial decisions

Eleven years across banking and retail have taught me one thing: analytics earns its place when it changes a decision. I started in BI and ETL, moved into data science, and for the last five years have led customer intelligence for Carrefour's loyalty programme at Majid Al Futtaim.

Today I own the analytics vision, governance and operating model for a 7M+ member base that influences AED 23B in annual sales. My team's segmentation, CLTV, churn and propensity models run live in Braze and on the web, personalising journeys every day. That work has delivered AED 300M+ in incremental sales at 10x ROI, validated by McKinsey.

I care as much about the system around the models as the models themselves: a team of data scientists, analysts and BI developers on one roadmap, self-serve tools so commercial teams don't wait on us, and a quarterly growth strategy I present to leadership in plain commercial language.
